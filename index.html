Description

Nestled in the charming Grove Terrace of Canterbury, this delightful mid-terrace house, built in 1870, offers a perfect blend of period character and modern comfort.

Upon entering, you are welcomed into a spacious sitting room, featuring a striking fireplace that serves as a focal point for the room. The separate dining room leads to a well-appointed kitchen, adorned with elegant navy units and a range cooker, providing ample space for a washing machine and fridge freezer.

The property boasts two generously sized double bedrooms, ensuring plenty of room for relaxation and rest. The large bathroom is a true highlight, featuring both a luxurious roll-top bath and a separate shower, catering to all your bathing needs.

This property also benefits from a dry cellar currently used as a workshop/gym and it has power and lighting.

Outside, the enclosed garden provides a private retreat, complete with a building that has an functioning outside toilet. Additionally, the property offers free on street parking a valuable asset in this desirable area.

This lovingly restored home, with its stripped wooden flooring and thoughtful design, is perfect for those seeking a blend of historical charm and contemporary living. With its proximity to local amenities and the vibrant culture of Canterbury, this property is an excellent opportunity for both first-time buyers and those looking to invest in a beautiful home.
